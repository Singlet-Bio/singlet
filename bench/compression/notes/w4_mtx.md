## 3. Test 4: raw/matrix.mtx versus the .1pz (tools/w4_mtx_t4.py, w4_mtx_t4_summary.py; results/w4_mtx_t4_*.json, w4_mtx_t4_summary.tsv)

I have no data on whether larger/deeper samples keep the same fractions: S4 is the check (end of file).

## 4. Anomaly worth a ticket (found while doing T4): ARC protocol samples' .1pz look strand-reversed
S1 (protocol_name `10x-arc-gex`, 7 of 467 sampled samples use this whitelist): only 3-4% of STARsolo nonzeros appear in the .1pz gene matrix. For the top cell GCTTTACGTTATCCGT: STARsolo MALAT1 456, TTN 423, NEAT1 80; the exon .1pz has TALAM1 427, TTN-AS1 109, MALAT1 43, TTN 24 and the intron .1pz has TTN-AS1 515: the counts land on the antisense genes. Consistent with an inverted read strand for this protocol in the pileup/export step (summary.json also says `status: align_low_map`, intergenic_fraction 0.87). Checked on ONE sample and ONE cell only; not verified further. The STARsolo output looks correct.
