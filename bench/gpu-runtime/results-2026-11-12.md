Job 812144 (K=4 T=6, defaults) did not finish a sample in 61 minutes: from minute 4 the first two samples of each GPU sat in kernel
direct compaction (their `gpu-records` threads at 100 % system time, memory pressure "full" 98 %, 49 failed compaction stalls and
400,000 failed page migrations per second, MemFree 7.6 GB of 750). It was cancelled. The same arm repeated from the same page-cache
state (812168) finished, with compaction stalls and near-zero free memory; with `SINGLET_NOINIT_HUGEPAGE=0` (812170) there were none.
`NoInitAlloc` (`include/singlet/engine_gpu.h`) asks for huge pages on every array of 64 MB or more.

## Readings

- A fourth sample in flight adds nothing (whole run 5.2-5.3 at K=4, 5.3-5.6 at K=3) and puts the node at the memory cliff above.
- CUDA MPS adds nothing; more threads add about 4 %; the two patches that shorten device-lock holds add 2-3 %. A phase made shorter
  gives the time back in another phase (all-site 44 -> 33 s, mapping 102 -> 113 s in 812451): the samples in flight compete for the
  host (43-45 of 54 cores busy on average), not for the device (a kernel runs in 55-62 % of seconds).
- NUMA binding is the largest single gain (4-6 %, and 7 % fewer core-s per pair); it needs no code change.
- CPU per sample at K=3 T=8 NUMA-bound with both patches (2,040 core-s seen): pipeline process 1,219 (end-of-run stages 440, during
  mapping 250, all-site 160, scan 141, QC 123, pileup 105), STAR child 448, T1K fastq-extractor 190, T1K genotyper 173. By thread name
  (2 s samples, 39 % of the CPU seen, the rest is in threads that live under 2 s): `sg-hla` 203, `gpu-verify` 110, `sg-export` 91 per
  sample. HLA typing as a whole (prefilter thread and the two T1K processes) is about 570 core-s per sample; these runs typed 0 donors.

## Correction: the campaign's pipeline arguments (from job 813025 on)

Every arm above ran without the campaign's pipeline arguments (`singlet-agents` `hpc/clipper/campaign/config.py`, `pipeline_args`):
`--hla-t1k <t1k-p3b>` (the patched T1K) and `--no-editing`. They typed HLA with the unpatched T1K of `<ref-base>/hla/t1k` and wrote the
RNA-editing matrices, so their rates are low and HLA's share of the CPU is overstated (T1K's two programs: 360 core-s per sample
there, 99 with the patched build). Comparisons between those arms hold, since they shared the arguments. The arms below pass them
(`PROD=1`); their output set hash is `72f93700fb04e419903b12153beaa938` (32 files per run). K=3, T=8, 24 of 24 runs in each.

- The prefilter inside the ingest (`pf3`) takes 120 core-s off a sample (7 %) and no time: its scan is now on the sample's critical
  path (the scan phase 23 -> 32 s, the mapping phase 98 -> 87 s). At 16 ingest threads the scan phase is 23 s again.
- HLA off is worth 7.5 % of the rate and 22 % of the CPU at this shape.
- From `pf3` on the node has idle cores at K=3 (37-39 of 54 busy): a sample's own length bounds the rate.
