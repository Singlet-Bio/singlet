\caption{\textbf{Reading over a network: decoder speed, round trips, latency and chunk size.}
\textbf{(a)} Size against the stored bits each reader consumes per second, $D_p b$, on \nm{threadsHeadline} cores; a
format is network-bound on every link below its point. Large points, medians; small, single matrices; squares, formats
that cannot be read in part. Shaded: under 8 bits per non-zero and fast enough for 1\,Gbit/s.
\textbf{(b)} Seconds to the first 512-cell minibatch at 1 and 30\,ms round trips (median over four datasets); the slope
counts the round trips paid in sequence.
\textbf{(c)} Time per minibatch at a 30\,ms round trip and 1\,Gbit/s, \nm{ns-dataset} (60 minibatches after 5 warm-up).
\textbf{(d)} The \sone{} reader with 1 to 64 requests at once and up to 8 minibatches ahead: share of the 1\,Gbit/s link
used, at 30 and 100\,ms.
\textbf{(e)} Three matrices re-encoded with chunk budgets of 4,096 to 393,216 non-zeros: bytes a random minibatch fetches
over the cells' share of the file. Chunks of 393,216 change the size of \nm{ns-dataset} by
\nm{chunk-hlca_core-393216-size}\% and raise this to \nm{chunk-hlca_core-393216-overread}.}
