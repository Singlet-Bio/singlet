\begin{table}[h]
\centering\small
\caption{What a stored count matrix offers besides its size and read speed, as each format is specified (``opt.'':
available as an option that writers rarely enable). Reads in part over HTTP: a reader can fetch some cells from plain
object storage or a web server with range requests, with no server-side software. Integrity: checks a reader can apply
to the bytes it reads. Data digest: a hash of the matrix's content (values, names) that does not depend on how it was
encoded. Integer guarantee: the format or its writer rejects matrices that are not non-negative integer counts.}

Format & Reads in part over HTTP & Integrity & Data digest & Several matrices + cell table & Integer guarantee \\

h5ad, 10x HDF5, loom (HDF5) & with chunked datasets and an HDF5 range driver & Fletcher-32 per chunk (opt.) & no & yes (h5ad, loom) & no \\
AnnData Zarr & yes: one object per chunk & CRC32C codec (opt., Zarr v3) & no & yes & no \\
TileDB-SOMA & yes, through the TileDB library & checksum filters (opt.) & no & yes & no \\
Parquet COO & yes: row groups and pages & page CRC-32 (opt.) & no & no & no \\
