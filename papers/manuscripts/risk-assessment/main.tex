\begin{abstract}
We present a comprehensive risk register for Singlify~AI, adapted from standard
venture risk frameworks (McKinsey 3~Horizons, Bessemer SLAT, YC risk matrix).
Twenty-two risks are identified and rated on probability~(1--5), impact~(1--5),
and urgency (immediate / 6~months / 12~months+). Risks are organized into four
tiers: existential (any one can kill the company), critical (high probability of
significant damage), significant (material but manageable), and moderate (watch
and manage). The top risks by composite score are solo founder~(20), insufficient
runway~(20), no product-market fit validation~(15), and CZI achieving uniform
FASTQ reprocessing~(15).
\end{abstract}

\section{Tier 1: Existential Risks}
\label{sec:existential}

Any one of these risks can terminate the company.

\subsection{R1: CZI Achieves Uniform FASTQ Reprocessing}

\textbf{Probability:} 3/5 (within 2 years, accelerating with NVIDIA partnership).
\textbf{Impact:} 5/5 (eliminates primary technical moat).
\textbf{Urgency:} 12 months.

CZI + NVIDIA partnership (October 2025) explicitly mentions scaling biological
data processing to petabytes. CZI expanded from 2 to 5 species in one release,
is adding spatial data, and TranscriptFormer TF-Exemplar already trains on
5~species.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Build value layers above the data (tools, community,
intelligence API). Formalize CZI collaboration to become the \emph{supplier}
of reprocessed data rather than a competitor.

\subsection{R2: Zero Product-Market Fit}

\textbf{Probability:} 3/5 (literally zero validation data).
\textbf{Impact:} 5/5 (company fails).
\textbf{Urgency:} Immediate.

No paying customers, no LOIs, no free users, no usage data, no
willingness-to-pay surveys. The beachhead market (comp bio postdocs) currently
pays \$0 for data access tools.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Ship free beta immediately. Run 30+ customer discovery
interviews. Get 5+ LOIs before fundraising.

\subsection{R3: Solo Founder Risk}

\textbf{Probability:} 5/5 (permanent structural weakness).
\textbf{Impact:} 4/5.
\textbf{Urgency:} Immediate.

VC data consistently shows solo founders raise less capital at lower valuations
with higher failure rates. For biotech/health-tech, VCs expect a technical +
commercial leadership pair.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Recruit a co-founder with 5+ years biotech/SaaS
commercial experience. Offer 15--25\% equity.

\subsection{R4: Insufficient Runway}

\textbf{Probability:} 5/5 (arithmetic certainty).
\textbf{Impact:} 4/5.
\textbf{Urgency:} Immediate.

Founder's own budget shows \$540K--\$1.06M Year~1 cost versus \$500K raise.
Zero buffer for anything going wrong.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Raise \$750K--\$1M, or cut scope drastically (no clinical
PGI, no Epic---just atlas + API + 2 hires).

\section{Tier 2: Critical Risks}
\label{sec:critical}

\subsection{R5: CZI Virtual Cell Initiative Leapfrogs}

Probability 3/5, Impact 4/5, Urgency 12+ months. CZI's Virtual Cell program
with NVIDIA, GREmLN, rBio1, and TranscriptFormer is building the same vision
with $\sim$1000$\times$ more resources.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Don't compete on virtual cell / foundation models.
Compete on data completeness and interpretability.

\subsection{R6: Cellarium AI Captures Cell Annotation Market}

Probability 3/5, Impact 3/5, Urgency 6 months. Cell Annotation Service is
exactly Singlify's community tools value proposition. Broad Institute backing.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Differentiate on breadth (200+ species, 6 modalities,
splicing) where Cellarium focuses on human/mouse RNA.

\subsection{R7: Academic $\to$ Commercial Execution Gap}

Probability 3/5, Impact 4/5, Urgency 6 months. Faculty startup failure mode
well-documented.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Announce leave of absence. Set hard external deadlines.
Hire operator/co-founder.

\subsection{R8: Tempus Adds Single-Cell (Acquisition)}

Probability 2/5, Impact 5/5, Urgency 12+ months. Tempus has \$632M revenue
and could acquire a single-cell data company for $<$\$50M.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Also an opportunity---be the acquisition target.

\subsection{R9: TileDB + CELLxGENE Deepen Integration}

Probability 4/5, Impact 3/5, Urgency 12 months. TileDB-SOMA powers Census.
Databricks partnership signals integrated platform ambitions.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Build on TileDB-SOMA / AnnData standards. Be part of
the ecosystem.

\subsection{R10: GPU Open-Source Tools Close Performance Gap}

Probability 4/5, Impact 3/5, Urgency 6 months. rapids-singlecell brings
GPU acceleration to open-source workflows.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Moat is data completeness, not performance.

\section{Tier 3: Significant Risks}
\label{sec:significant}

\subsection{R11: Foundation Model Commoditization}

Probability 4/5, Impact 3/5. Open weights for Geneformer, scGPT, UCE, scVI.
CELLxGENE hosts 7+ model embeddings. NMF is also open-source.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Position as training data supplier, not foundation model
company.

\subsection{R12: Pharma Builds Internally}

Probability 3/5, Impact 3/5. Recursion, Insitro, Genentech have \$100M+
internal capabilities.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Target mid-tier pharma/biotech (\$50M--\$5B market cap).

\subsection{R13: AlphaGenome Licensing Never Opens}

Probability 3/5, Impact 2/5. Google has not committed commercial licensing.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Already trunk-agnostic. Lead with Borzoi (Apache 2.0).

\subsection{R14: Free-Tool Culture Prevents Monetization}

Probability 4/5, Impact 3/5. Scanpy, Seurat, CELLxGENE---all free. 2--5\%
freemium conversion rates.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Academic free / commercial paid model. Free for .edu.
Paid for pharma/biotech.

\subsection{R15: Pricing Wrong}

Probability 3/5, Impact 3/5. \$49/mo may be too high for postdocs and too low
for pharma.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Run Gabor-Granger or Van Westendorp pricing study.

\subsection{R16: Clinical PGI Timeline Destroys Credibility}

Probability 4/5, Impact 3/5. FDA 510(k) + Epic on 18-month timeline with no
clinical staff is not credible.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Remove clinical PGI from pre-seed pitch. Focus entirely
on research data platform.

\subsection{R17: Community Tools Don't Get Adoption}

Probability 3/5, Impact 2/5. Zero community developers currently.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Seed ecosystem with 3--5 internal tools first.

%% ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Tier 4: Moderate Risks}
\label{sec:moderate}

\subsection{R18: Data Quality at Scale}

Probability 3/5, Impact 2/5. One high-profile quality issue could damage trust.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Publish per-dataset QC metrics. Community reporting.

\subsection{R19: Cloud Provider Offers Pre-Processed Data}

Probability 2/5, Impact 3/5. AWS already mirrors GEO on S3.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Be cloud-agnostic. Use Cloudflare R2 as primary hosting.

\subsection{R20: Regulatory Risk for Clinical Applications}

Probability 3/5, Impact 3/5. FDA scrutiny of AI/ML in clinical genomics
increasing.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Defer clinical applications to Series~A+.

\subsection{R21: University IP Conflict}

Probability 2/5, Impact 4/5. Universities typically claim IP for work using
university resources.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Get written IP assignment/release from GVSU before
fundraising.

\subsection{R22: Data Licensing Complications}

Probability 2/5, Impact 3/5. GEO data has varying licensing terms.

\textbf{Mitigation:} Audit licensing for all 19,790 series. Build licensing
metadata into catalog.
