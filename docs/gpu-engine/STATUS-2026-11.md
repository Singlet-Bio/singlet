Written 2026-11-04 on branch `perf/gpu-next`. Sources are the branches and pull requests named below, `docs/gpu-engine/exploration.md`, `docs/gpu-engine/fable/HANDOFF.md`, the beta pipeline review and the launch notes in singlet-agents.

## 4. What is blocked, and why

- **Fable cannot merge as is.** It conflicts with `prod/campaign` in ten files. Campaign changed output definitions, so the CPU references are stale.
- **Two device dedups exist.** E12 on Fable and #145 on campaign do the same work. They are not additive.
- **E21 pool fails on hgmm1k_v3.** STAR exits 102 about 30 s in. The barnyard genome differs from the others. The resident genome is the first suspect.
- **Large samples are not gated.** The largest gated sample is 118.7 M reads. GSM7656149 has 263 M reads. GSM7064234 has 1.2 G pairs.
- **Drain worker memory model.** It was fitted before the ingest. It needs a refit.
- **Level 5 and the extra outputs.** `SeqCache.h` states that its exactness assumes no chimeric detection. Chimeric, TE and viral outputs were not verified at level 5.
- **`.1fg2` route.** The table-fed prefill and E13's `.1fq` ingest do not combine. This branch keeps the table prefill only below E10 level 2.
- **g050.** Its GPU fails CUDA initialization. Gates exclude it.
- **Exon multimapper bug.** It touches `k_resolve`. It is not part of this work.

## 6. Results of this work

All runs are on Clipper H100 NVL nodes with real data. The reference is the CPU engine of the same binary on the same sample. The gate is out_equiv over 65 identical files plus the run-dependent set (timings, paths, read order). Job logs are under `$WORK/logs/gn-*` and results under `$WORK/jobs/gpu-next/results/` on Clipper. The scripts are in `bench/gpu_next/jobs/`.
